What a Report Card actually looks like.

$50K Estimated annual revenue being lost

We analyzed a Caribbean restaurant in Brampton — a well-loved local spot with great food and a loyal customer base. But their Uber Eats menu had duplicate listings, no descriptions, underpriced items, and zero upsell strategy. Customers were confused. Non-Caribbean customers were skipping unfamiliar dishes entirely.

Our report identified 7 specific issues and delivered a complete menu rewrite with vivid descriptions, strategic pricing adjustments, and a new upsell item (Homemade Scotch Bonnet Sauce, $1.50). The full fix cost them under $200 to implement and is projected to recover $35,000–$50,000 in annual revenue.
