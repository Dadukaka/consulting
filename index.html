What We Do

Tailored strategies for real growth

Every restaurant is different. We start by understanding your specific challenges, then craft a strategy that fits your concept, market, and goals — no generic playbooks.

01
Business Health Check

A deep dive into your competitive landscape, customer sentiment, and operational strengths — identifying the opportunities most restaurants overlook.

02
Menu Audit & Optimization

Using menu psychology and data-driven analysis, we redesign your offerings to improve average order value, reduce complexity, and drive repeat visits.

03
Delivery Platform Strategy

We optimize your Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip The Dishes presence — from photography guidance and item descriptions to pricing and ranking tactics.

Our Approach

Strategy built on observation, not assumption

We don't rely on spreadsheets alone. APRA Consulting combines on-site observation, customer behavior research, and platform analytics to give you a complete picture of where your restaurant stands — and a clear path to where it can go.
